# Briefs you can give your agent

Start each session by asking the agent to read `AGENTS.md` and the MOTIVO skill. Replace paths with real supplied files. These prompts are examples, not generated customer work.

Not sure what to make? Ask “Tunjukkan senarai jenis video MOTIVO” or open the [video-type catalog](VIDEO_TYPES.md).

## Short drama / sketch

> Buat drama pendek komedi 45 saat tentang ayah dan anak berebut remote. Dua watak, satu ruang tamu, Bahasa Melayu natural. Sediakan logline, skrip, nota watak dan shotlist dahulu. Bimbing aku pilih rakaman sendiri, drama bergambar atau klip Nexabot. Jangan jana aset berbayar dulu.

> Buat drama bergambar misteri 30 saat daripada public/assets/misteri/. Guna narator Google AI Studio manual; sediakan teks dan arahan suara. Gunakan bunyi serta potongan yang membina ketegangan. Tiada dakwaan lip-sync; jangan reka rakaman pelakon yang tidak dibekalkan.

## Faceless essay

> Explain why urban trees reduce heat. Audience: general Malaysian viewers. 45 seconds, 9:16, Bahasa Melayu. Research factual claims and keep source URLs in project notes. Use my photos and narration; no generated documentary evidence. Develop a paper-and-ink art direction with precise diagrams, varied shot lengths and calm captions.

## Product detail

> Turn public/assets/bottle/ into a 30-second affiliate video. Use only specs.md for claims. My voice is narration.wav. Feature real close-ups, deliberate crops, clear feature labels and a qualified CTA. No fake use demonstration, invented discount or personal testimonial. Add the affiliate disclosure I supplied in brief.txt.

## Comparison

> Compare these two keyboards for office work, using the supplied specification sheets. Show tradeoffs with clear criteria. Do not invent a winner or current prices. 40 seconds, landscape. Suggest a direction that makes the differences visible without repeating dashboard cards.

## Visual explainer

> Explain how a bicycle gear ratio works in 35 seconds. Use meaningful moving geometry and a worked numerical example; label units. Design in charcoal and chartreuse. No stock footage or external API. Build a custom layout, not a recolored study.

## Place & culture

> Build a 60-second photo essay about Melaka with my licensed photos and recorded narration. Verify dates before writing. Use Malay captions and deliberate editorial typography. Keep generated illustration out of documentary claims. Preserve the source ledger.

## Optional generated host

> Plan a realistic Malay host review using my authorized identity reference and product photos. First show the shotlist, generation plan and provider cost estimate if verifiable. Do not submit paid generation yet. Keep natural speech, modest clothing, product geometry and consistent wardrobe. Flag any shot the provider cannot render reliably.
